How to Play on 2 PCs on Same LAN & Voice Guide

Simple setup for 2-player LAN chess & hands-free voice control

Way 1: Instant 4-Digit Room Code (Zero Setup)

1. Open this app on PC 1 and click PC 1: Host Room (White). You will get a 4-digit Room Code.
2. Open this app on PC 2, type that 4-digit Room Code, and click Join (Black).
Both PCs connect peer-to-peer over your local network. White speaks moves on PC 1, and Black speaks moves on PC 2!

Way 2: 100% Offline LAN Server (`python3 lan_server.py`)

If your LAN has no internet at all, extract voice_chess_lan.zip on PC 1 and run:
python3 lan_server.py Then open http://<PC1-LAN-IP>:8000 on both PCs — moves sync automatically across your LAN!

Corrected Multi-Alternative Speech Recognition

The voice engine now checks up to 5 speech alternatives per utterance, splits merged numbers (like when "E2 to E4" is heard as "E22 E4" or "G12 F3"), corrects misheard letters ("I2 से I4" → "E2 to E4"), and pauses the mic while speaking aloud so it never hears its own voice.
